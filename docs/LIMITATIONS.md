# Limitations and responsible-use constraints

PolicyGuard is entirely synthetic and is for portfolio demonstration only. Its templated notes and rule-generated labels may create relationships that are substantially clearer than operational insurance data. Metrics are not external validation and do not predict business value.

The application has no customer-identifying data, credentials, or external data sources. Before any real deployment, an insurer would need data-governance approval, consent and privacy controls, protected-class and proxy-feature analysis, model-risk approval, calibration and temporal validation, human review, intervention experimentation, monitoring, and a documented appeals/escalation process.
