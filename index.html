Responsible portfolio use

Synthetic by design.

Every record, service note, label, and metric in PolicyGuard is synthetic. This project demonstrates technical approaches and must not be used for underwriting, pricing, claims, customer eligibility, or real retention decisions without validation, governance, privacy controls, and fairness review.
